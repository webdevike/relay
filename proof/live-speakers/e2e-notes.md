## Topics Discussed
### Product positioning and design stages
- The product should be trendy, appeal to a wide market and be user friendly, including for grannies and kids.
- Functional design: which needs the product must fill and how.
- Conceptual design: what the team is thinking.

### Animal drawing warm-up
- Ted drew a tiger and described it as majestic.
- David drew a sitting dog: supportive, loyal, with intuition.
- Priya's drawing started as a T Rex, became a pelican, and ended as a crocodile described as strong.
- Marco drew an eagle: independent, free spirited, intrepid.
- Marco had trouble with a pen coming apart.

### Finance
- Selling price of 25 euros, which David and Priya estimated at about 17 pounds.
- Priya said similar products on the market cost about 20 pounds.
- Marco asked whether undercutting the price would make the product look cheap.
- Production cost of 12.50 euros, half the selling price.
- Profit aim of 50 million euros in the first year.

### Market
- International market, including Australia.
- Not focused on business users; aimed at everyone and all age groups.
- Ted did not think different languages were a concern.

### Universal remotes
- Ted described a universal remote that you program for each device. It kept getting lost and seemed cheap, but integrating everything into one was good.
- Marco said he has only used the remote that came with a purchased device and never programmed one.

## Decisions
