## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-42 | Elevation | job name into spawn argv | mitigate | contract JobName regex, existence check via listJobs, argv array, no shell |
| T-03-43 | Tampering | stale form overwriting a newer job.md | mitigate | update requires expected basis equality inside EvaFs.edit; refusal never retries |
| T-03-44 | Tampering | delete removing run history or escaping jobs/ | mitigate | remove('job-delete') limited by allowlist to jobs/<name>; jobs/.runs untouched |
| T-03-45 | Denial of service | killing the runner | mitigate | detached and unref'd; no kill calls (acceptance grep) |
| T-03-46 | Denial of service | steps payload size | mitigate | contract caps steps and basis at 200 KB |
