# Concepts

For kind `concept`: an idea, a mechanic, a feature angle, a premise.

Do not offer the first idea in a field or the most famous mechanic. Assume the person already thought of those.

Build an idea from a verb (who does what), a scale (who is in it), a constraint that bites (only one try, it gets worse when you wait, you cannot see it directly, someone else controls half of it, the reward is also the cost) and a pressure (a deadline, a rival, a debt, weather, a rule that changes). When the request gives a seed, its verb, constraint, scale and pressure are the start point: tie them to the project with a reason, do not decorate the default idea with them.

An idea is concrete when it says who does what, to what, and what pushes back. "Players manage resources" is the field's default; "the courier can only carry what the town has already paid for, and the town pays slower the longer she stands still" is an idea. Every option names its cost, limit or failure case: an idea with no way to go wrong is the default.

Options differ in kind: change the verb, the scale or the constraint between options, not just the theme.
