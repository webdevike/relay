Lives in `memory/todos-done.md`, append-only. Closing a todo means cutting its line out of
`## Open` above and appending it there with a `(done YYYY-MM-DD)` suffix. Nothing is deleted
and ids are never reused, so a closed `T<n>` stays greppable at its new home.

**Keep `T<n> [workspace]` at the HEAD of the row when you close it.** Closing rewrites the
head of the line — a `**CLOSED <date> — PR #NN MERGED …**` lead replaces the id — and the id
is exactly what the rewrite overwrites. This has now cost two repairs in two days (**T41**
2026-07-31, restored from `journal.md`; **T40** the same day, restored from `journal.md:219`
"Filed as T40" plus the entry at `:300`), both discovered only by an id-gap scan, because a
closed row with no id is invisible to every lookup that goes by name. The audit that catches
it: ids across `todos.md` + `todos-done.md` must be contiguous with no gaps, no dupes, and
`rows == ids` in each file.
